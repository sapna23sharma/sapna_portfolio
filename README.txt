Sapna Portfolio Template

How to edit:
1. Open content.js
2. Update your name, summary, about section, projects, experience, education, and contact links.
3. Replace images inside the assets folder with your own screenshots and profile photo.
4. Open index.html in a browser to preview.

The colors, layout, gradients, and animations are controlled by styles.css, so you can update your content without disturbing the theme.
